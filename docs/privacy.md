# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 7, 2026*

ARcade Space is made by Inner Space XR Studio. **We do not collect, store or share any of your personal data.**

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** The app has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us.
- **Camera:** used only on your device to place cabinets in augmented reality. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Photos:** the app only adds images or videos to your photo library when you tap the capture button and choose to save them. It never reads your library.
- **Files:** game files you import and your save states stay on your device.
- **Game Center:** if you're signed in to Game Center, your high scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
